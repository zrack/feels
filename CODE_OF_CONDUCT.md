# Code of Conduct for feels

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming emotional climate, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of how they are currently feeling.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language about feelings
- Being respectful of differing emotional weather systems
- Gracefully accepting that someone else’s feel is valid even if it differs from yours
- Focusing on what is best for the collective mood of the repository

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling or insulting other people’s genuine feels
- Public or private harassment based on emotional expression
- Publishing others’ private emotional information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a space dedicated to feelings

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the repository owner. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated with as much care as the feels themselves deserve.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, but made slightly softer around the edges because this is a feels repo.
