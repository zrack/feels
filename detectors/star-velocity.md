# Star Velocity Detector

**Method:**

Observe the rate at which stars are being added to the repository.

- Sudden influx of stars → the repo is feeling seen / slightly overwhelmed
- Slow steady trickle → quiet validation
- Long periods of zero new stars → contemplative solitude or mild existential dread
- Stars being removed (rare) → complex feelings we do not yet have language for

No formal thresholds. Use your judgment. Document the reading in the feels log if it feels significant.
